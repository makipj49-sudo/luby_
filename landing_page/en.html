Ruby Kiwi Benefits

Unique Red Hue

Ruby kiwis are rich in anthocyanins, natural antioxidants, giving them a ruby-red center. Their visual beauty makes them popular gifts.

Incredible Sweetness

They have less acidity and much higher sugar content than regular kiwis. Characterized by deep sweetness and a soft texture.

Nutritional Powerhouse

Extremely high in Vitamin C, dietary fiber, and folic acid, making them excellent for health management.

Premium Ruby Mango

Golden flesh and ruby skin, experience the unique value of the Queen of Mangoes, the Ruby Mango.

Peak of Apple Mango

The deep golden flesh hidden inside the red skin, which looks like a ripe apple, captures both sight and taste.

Silk-like Texture

With almost no fiber, it melts in your mouth with its softness and rich juice.

Overwhelming Brix

With an average of over 20 Brix, it's sweeter than sugar and leaves a long-lasting, deep flavor.
